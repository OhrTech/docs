**Our promise:** within `v1`, the API only adds: new operations, new optional fields, new values.
Anything else becomes `v2`, served alongside `v1`. The OpenAPI document's history is in the
[management-api repository](https://github.com/OhrTech/management-api/commits/main/openapi.json).

## 2026-11-11: Unmanaged overrides a fleet

- A device's settings may give a setting `{"mode": "Unmanaged"}`: the device leaves it alone even
  when a fleet manages it. `null` still removes the override, so the device follows its fleets. Until
  now, `Unmanaged` in a request was treated as `null`. See [settings](/management-api/guides/settings/).

## 2026-11-10: web apps and web push

[Web apps](/web-apps/): your websites as apps on your devices.

- **Web apps:** `POST /v1/web-apps/preview` shows what a site's manifest gives;
  `POST /v1/orgs/{orgId}/web-apps` makes a web app; `GET` and `PATCH /v1/apps/{appId}/web` read and
  change its mode, display, scope rule and packaged policy; `GET /v1/apps/{appId}/web/manifest-changes`
  and `POST /v1/apps/{appId}/web/manifest-changes/approve` take the manifest's new name and icon;
  `GET /v1/orgs/{orgId}/web-signing-key` gives your organisation's web app certificate. See
  [make a web app](/web-apps/create/).
- **Packaged web apps:** `POST`, `GET /v1/apps/{appId}/web/bundles` and
  `DELETE /v1/apps/{appId}/web/bundles/{version}` for Signed Web Bundles; `GET /v1/apps/{appId}/web/versions`.
  See [packaged web apps](/web-apps/packaged/).
- **Push:** a message in `POST /v1/push` may give `subscription`, a web app's push subscription, in
  place of `token`. See [notifications and push](/web-apps/notifications-and-push/).
- OhrTech.Management 2.4.0 has all of these, with `WebPushSubscription`,
  `PushSealer.SealForSubscription` and `DeclarativeWebPush`; ohr 1.1.0 adds `ohr push`.

## 2026-11-08: the Device SDK's server side

For apps that use the [Device SDK](/device-sdk/) on your devices:

- **Access:** which Device SDK areas an app may change on a fleet's devices: `GET`, `PUT` and
  `DELETE /v1/fleets/{fleetId}/apps/{appId}/sdk-access`. `GET /v1/devices/{deviceId}/events` lists
  what apps changed, what the Agent refused, and apps' own events.
- **Push:** `POST /v1/push` sends up to 500 sealed, signed messages and needs the new `SendPush`
  permission; `GET /v1/push/{id}` follows one; `GET /v1/devices/{deviceId}/push-tokens` lists a
  device's tokens. See [Send push from your server](/device-sdk/server/send-push/).
- **Attestation:** `POST /v1/attestations/verify`, `GET /v1/attestations/root` and
  `GET /v1/attestations/status` (no sign-in), `POST /v1/devices/{deviceId}/attestation/revoke`, and
  `POST /v1/devices/{deviceId}/key-attestations/verify` for an app's TEE keys. See
  [Verify attestations](/device-sdk/server/verify-attestation/).
- **Managed configuration:** `GET /v1/apps/{appId}/config-schema`, and `GET`, `PUT` and `DELETE` on
  `/v1/fleets/{fleetId}/apps/{appId}/config` and `/v1/devices/{deviceId}/apps/{appId}/config`.
- **Kiosk:** `POST /v1/devices/{deviceId}/kiosk/exit` lifts kiosk mode for some minutes without the
  exit PIN.
- **Crash reports and logs:** `GET /v1/apps/{appId}/crashes`, `/v1/apps/{appId}/crashes/{signature}`
  and `/v1/devices/{deviceId}/crashes`; `POST` and `GET /v1/devices/{deviceId}/log-bundles`.
- **USB devices:** `GET`, `PUT` and `DELETE /v1/fleets/{fleetId}/apps/{appId}/usb-devices`, and
  `GET /v1/devices/{deviceId}/usb-devices` for what is attached.
- **Certificates for apps:** issuing CAs under `/v1/orgs/{orgId}/issuing-cas`, an app's certificates
  under `/v1/fleets/{fleetId}/apps/{appId}/certificates`, `GET /v1/devices/{deviceId}/certificates`, and
  `GET /v1/device-identity/root` (no sign-in). See [Client certificates](/device-sdk/server/client-certificates/).

## 2026-11-06: what apps may do on a device

- Apps the hub installs get the runtime and special-access permissions they request automatically,
  and a battery-optimization exemption when they declare `REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS`.
- Privileged permissions are granted once OhrTech approves them for the organisation (or for one of
  OhrTech's apps): `GET /v1/apps/{appId}/permissions` shows each one's state, and
  `GET /v1/orgs/{orgId}/permission-approvals` what OhrTech approved. OhrTech staff approve with
  `PUT` and withdraw with `DELETE` on `/v1/orgs/{orgId}/permission-approvals/{permission}` and
  `/v1/apps/{appId}/permission-approvals/{permission}`.
- microG Services and microG Companion are catalog apps: Google Play services' push, sign-in and
  location API for apps written against them.

## 2026-11-05: when devices install, and OhrTech's apps

- Each fleet chooses when its devices install app updates: `GET`, `PUT` and `DELETE
  /v1/fleets/{fleetId}/update-timing` (when the screen is off, the default; a maintenance window;
  either; or immediately). Devices download enforced versions at once and install them then. An
  app a device doesn't have yet installs at once, whatever the timing.
- Apps have a `kind`: `org` (yours), `catalog` (provided by OhrTech, such as the Vanadium browser) or
  `platform` (part of the OS, such as Vanadium System WebView). `orgId` and `orgName` are null for
  OhrTech's apps; versions have `notes` and `security`.
- Per fleet, OhrTech's apps come automatically or only once approved: `GET`, `PUT` and `DELETE
  /v1/fleets/{fleetId}/apps/{appId}/policy`.
- Available versions download ahead over Wi-Fi. A device that leaves a fleet, or a fleet you take an
  app from, uninstalls the app.
- Rollouts: the statuses `held` and `waiting`, and `targetVersionCode`, `policy` per device; `held`
  and `waiting` counts.

## 2026-11-04: ids with a type prefix

The one change to `v1` that is not an addition, made the day after it was published:

- Every id is a string whose prefix says what it names, such as `dev_3swt6f457299btzpw1bxx4wf0q`: `org_` an
  organisation, `flt_` a fleet, `dev_` a device, `app_` an app, `tsk_` a task, `usr_` a person,
  `sa_` a service account, `adb_` and `rc_` remote ADB and remote control sessions. Ids were
  numbers, and sessions UUIDs.
- Organisations are called that throughout: `orgId` and `orgName` where it said `clientId` and
  `clientName`, `orgs` in `GET /v1/me`, and the `orgId` filter.
- An id of another kind, such as a fleet's id where a device's belongs, is 400 ("Not a device id").
- The [.NET SDK](/management-api/sdks/dotnet/) 2.0.0 and [`ohr`](/management-api/cli/) use them.

## 2026-11-04: your apps

- [Your apps](/management-api/guides/apps/): `GET` and `POST /v1/apps`, `GET` and `PATCH
  /v1/apps/{appId}`, the app's versions (`GET /v1/apps/{appId}/versions`), uploading an APK as a new
  version (`POST /v1/apps/{appId}/versions`, refused if it is another package), holding, offering or
  enforcing a version (`PATCH /v1/apps/{appId}/versions/{versionCode}`), the rollout
  (`GET /v1/apps/{appId}/rollout`) and a fleet's apps (`GET`, `PUT` and `DELETE
  /v1/fleets/{fleetId}/apps/…`).
- The new permission `ManageApps`, held by every member, lets the caller change apps. Seeing them
  needs `ManageApps` or `ViewDevices`.
- The [`ohr` command line](/management-api/cli/) is open source, for Linux, macOS and Windows.

## 2026-11-03: shell commands

- `POST /v1/devices/{deviceId}/shell` runs a [shell command](/management-api/guides/shell-commands/)
  on a device, as `adb shell` would. It needs `RemoteAdb`. The finished task's `shell` holds the
  exit code and output.
- Tasks have `createdBy`: the person or service account that created them.
- Devices have `shellCommands` (in the list, and under `capabilities`) and, in the list,
  `securityPatch`.

## 2026-11-03: corrections

- `os.securityPatch` is the Android security patch level the device runs now, as `YYYY-MM-DD`,
  reported at every check-in. It was the level the device's enrolment attested, as `YYYYMM`, which
  did not follow OS updates.
- `takeOver`, when opening a remote control session, is optional (default `false`).

## 2026-11-03: v1

The first public version:

- Devices and fleets: live status, details, installed apps, enrolments, check-in on demand, moving
  devices between fleets
- Settings: the catalog, reading, `PUT` and `PATCH` with validation, pushing again
- Messages and tasks
- Remote control, with the embeddable viewer and its `postMessage` interface
- Remote ADB sessions
- Sign-in: service accounts with permissions, device scope, network allowlist and embed sites; and
  the device code flow for people
