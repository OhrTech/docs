Every OhrTech device checks in with OhrTech regularly and reports its state. The API gives you that
state, and lets you organise devices into **fleets**: groups that pass their settings down to their
devices and to the fleets below them.

## List devices

`GET /v1/devices` returns every device you can reach. Narrow it with `fleetId` (devices directly in
that fleet), `online=true` or `false` (checked in within the last 20 minutes), or `clientId` if you
belong to more than one organisation. Needs `ViewDevices`.

<Tabs syncKey="lang">
<TabItem label="curl">

```sh
curl "https://api.ohrtech.net/v1/devices?fleetId=7&online=true" -H "Authorization: Bearer $OHR_TOKEN"
```

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="C#">

```csharp
var devices = await api.ListDevicesAsync(fleetId: 7, online: true);
```

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

Each device comes with what you need for a dashboard: `serial`, `fleetName`, `online` and
`lastCheckIn`, `batteryLevel` and `charging`, `network` (`WiFi` or `Mobile`), `simOperator`,
`agentVersion` and `agentOutdated`, `settingsInSync` and `settingsErrors`, and whether it supports
`remoteControl` and `remoteAdb`.

## One device in detail

`GET /v1/devices/{deviceId}` adds:

| Section | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `agent` | The OhrTech Agent's version, the version offered to it, and whether it is behind |
| `os` | The Foundation OS build fingerprint, build ID and build date |
| `network` | The active network, Wi-Fi signal, mobile state, operator and signal, SIM operator and ICCID |
| `battery` | Level, status and whether it is charging |
| `settings` | The policy revision it must follow and the one it applied, whether they match, and how many settings failed |
| `capabilities` | Whether it supports remote control and remote ADB, and `yourPermissions` on it |

## Installed apps and enrolments

- `GET /v1/devices/{deviceId}/apps` lists the installed packages as the device last reported them:
  `package`, `name`, `version` and `versionCode`. Use it to check that your app is installed, at the
  right version, everywhere.
- `GET /v1/devices/{deviceId}/enrolments` lists every time the device enrolled, newest first. Each
  enrolment is backed by the device's hardware-rooted attestation; `kind: "re-enrolled"` means the
  device made new keys, which usually means it was factory reset.

## Ask a device to check in now

Devices check in on their own schedule. When you need fresh status (before a support call, after a
change), ask:

```sh
curl -X POST https://api.ohrtech.net/v1/devices/42/check-in -H "Authorization: Bearer $OHR_TOKEN"
```

The answer is `202 Accepted`. A reachable device checks in within seconds; read it again with
`GET /v1/devices/42`. A device on mobile data may only hear the request at its next scheduled
check-in.

## Fleets

`GET /v1/fleets` lists your fleets with `parentFleetId` and `deviceCount`. Fleets nest: a device
follows the settings of its fleet and of every fleet above it, top first, then its own overrides
(see [settings](/management-api/guides/settings/)).

### Move a device

Needs `MoveDevices`. The device gets its new fleet's settings at once.

<Tabs syncKey="lang">
<TabItem label="curl">

```sh
curl -X PUT https://api.ohrtech.net/v1/devices/42/fleet \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OHR_TOKEN" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"fleetId": 7}'
```

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="C#">

```csharp
await api.MoveDeviceAsync(42, new MoveDeviceRequest { FleetId = 7 });
```

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

`{"fleetId": null}` takes the device out of every fleet. `fleetId` is required, so a request that
leaves it out by mistake is refused instead of moving the device.

To sort many devices at once from your own inventory, see the
[sort into fleets](/management-api/recipes/sort-into-fleets/) recipe.
