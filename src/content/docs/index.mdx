## Where does your code run?

The OhrTech SDK has two parts. The **Management API** is for code that runs on your servers and
desktops and manages many devices at once. The **Device SDK** is for the app you ship on the device
itself.

<Products />

<p class="ohr-eyebrow">Management API</p>

## What you can build with it

<Features>
<Feature title="Remote support inside your own help desk" href="/management-api/guides/embed-remote-control/">

One call opens a session and returns a link you put in an `<iframe>`. Your support staff see the
device's screen and control it from your app, without an OhrTech Portal account. Your page hears
what happens (waiting, the person allowed it, connected, ended) and can press keys or end the
session.

</Feature>
<Feature title="Every device setting, with inheritance" href="/management-api/guides/settings/">

Wi-Fi networks, screen lock, brightness and volume, home screen layout, allowed keyboards, USB and
Bluetooth: nearly 50 settings, each set (the person may change it) or locked. Fleets pass them
down; a device can override. Every change is validated before it is stored, and you see what each
device reports back.

</Feature>
<Feature title="Fleets that follow your inventory" href="/management-api/recipes/sort-into-fleets/">

Move devices between fleets from your own systems, by serial number. A device moved into a fleet
gets that fleet's settings at once.

</Feature>
<Feature title="A shell on every device" href="/management-api/guides/shell-commands/">

Run any command you would type after `adb shell` on a device anywhere, from your own code, and get
its exit code and output back: open a settings screen, read a property, collect diagnostics from a
whole fleet at once. Every command is recorded with who ran it.

</Feature>
<Feature title="ADB from anywhere" href="/management-api/guides/remote-adb/">

Your engineers use their own `adb` and Android Studio on a device in a store, a vehicle or on
mobile data. The connection is end-to-end encrypted, and the device needs no open port or VPN.

</Feature>
<Feature title="Know before your users call" href="/management-api/recipes/fleet-health-alerts/">

Every device's last check-in, battery, network, OS build, installed apps and whether its settings
applied. Ask any device to check in now. Turn it into alerts, reports and audit evidence.

</Feature>
</Features>

## Made to pass your security review

- **A service account per integration.** Each has only the permissions you grant it, can be limited
  to particular fleets or devices, to the networks it may call from, and to the web sites that may
  show its remote control sessions.
- **Every action on the record.** Each task, such as a shell command or a message, keeps who
  created it, and each remote session when it opened and how it ended.
- **Checked on every call.** A token only says who is calling. Remove a permission, disable an
  account or lock a person out, and the next call fails, not the next hour's.
- **Secrets stay put.** Wi-Fi passwords and screen lock codes go in and never come back out.
- **People sign in as themselves.** Tools your staff run use the device code flow, with the OhrTech
  Portal's own sign-in and two-factor authentication.
